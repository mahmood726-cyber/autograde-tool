AutoGRADE v1.0.0

Open-Source Certainty of Evidence Calculator

Computes GRADE certainty ratings from quantitative meta-analysis parameters. An open-source, browser-based tool that automates the 5 downgrade domains and 3 upgrade domains.

GRADE Domains

Downgrade (each -1 or -2):

  1. Risk of Bias: proportion of studies at high/unclear RoB, weighted by contribution
  2. Inconsistency: I² thresholds (>40% serious, >75% very serious) + direction
  3. Indirectness: user-assessed (population, intervention, comparator, outcome)
  4. Imprecision: OIS not met + CI crosses null or clinical threshold
  5. Publication Bias: Egger's p < 0.10 + small study count

Upgrade (observational only, each +1):

  1. Large effect: OR < 0.5 or > 2.0
  2. Dose-response: gradient present
  3. Plausible confounding: would reduce the observed effect

Thresholds Used

  • RoB: >30% high → serious; >50% high → very serious (AutoGRADE heuristic — GRADE recommends weight-proportional assessment)
  • I²: 0-40% not serious; 40-75% serious; >75% very serious
  • Imprecision: CI crosses null → serious; CI crosses null AND OIS not met → very serious
  • Pub bias: Egger p < 0.10 AND k < 10 → serious; Egger p < 0.05 → very serious

Output

Overall: HIGH / MODERATE / LOW / VERY LOW. Plus a Summary of Findings table with absolute effects and NNT.

Limitations

  • GRADE inherently involves judgment — this tool automates the quantitative components but cannot replace domain expertise for indirectness and some RoB assessments
  • Thresholds are based on published GRADE guidance but may differ from panel-specific decisions
  • OIS computation uses simplified formula (not trial sequential analysis)
